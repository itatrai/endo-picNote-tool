How saving works

Photos you take with the camera icon are kept inside this app and named PARTNUMBER_date_time.jpg, so they sort together by part number. When you tap a Save button, the iPhone share sheet opens: choose Save to Files, then your OneDrive folder (it remembers the last folder). With the ZIP option, unzipping gives one folder per part number. Save regularly and only delete photos here once you have checked them in OneDrive.
